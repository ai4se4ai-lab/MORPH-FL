## Five-sentence comprehension summary

MORPH-FL investigates budgeted Python fault localization using a report, retrieved source, sequential model roles, and permitted checks. It stores observations separately from hypothesis interpretations and dialogue, then may clear dialogue and prune hypotheses when an explicit plateau policy requests a reset. Static checks support limited source or structural predictions, while cached and handled identities can suppress reassessment or leave recreated hypotheses without their former evidence links. File ranking, symbol ranking, early stopping, and causal abstention follow distinct rules, so retained observations and favorable ranks do not establish a causal diagnosis. The planned comparisons study this complete policy and selected component variants, with explicit limits on attribution to typed formatting, psychological fixation, or useful reconstructed context.
