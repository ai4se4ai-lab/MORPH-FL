Draft displays every key visibly. Final generator must reject absent keys/incomplete/smoke inputs and read full analyze.py/extended_analysis outputs with source/protocol provenance. Macros: res, resrange, claimslot, figslot, tabslot.

Numeric keys: file-top1-delta (paired MORPH-control patch-proxy hit difference; seeds averaged/case); file-top1-ci (repository-cluster95% interval); function-mrr-delta (first exact-function proxy reciprocal-rank difference, secondary descriptive); recovery-delta (proxy-wrong AND first-valid leader followed clue, final proxy hit; denominator disclosed); retention (before/after reset observation-ID set audit); tokens (actual completed-case input+output, interrupted costs separate); probes (physical executions); wall-seconds (recorded controller boundary, no end-to-end speed claim). Primary file Top1 sign-flip/Holm family declared in protocol; other measures descriptive unless independently preregistered. Empty eligible denominators -> unavailable.

Claim keys and ALL directional variants:
- effectiveness: favorable supported contrasts -> “The study identifies proxy-localization benefits under the reported conditions.” unfavorable -> “The controls yield stronger proxy-localization rankings under the reported conditions.” mixed/uncertain -> “The study does not establish a consistent proxy-localization advantage.”
- mechanism: eligible recovery and coherent component contrasts favorable -> “The trajectories support the tested restart mechanism under the disclosed proxy definitions.” unfavorable -> “The tested restart state does not justify its cost under these conditions.” mixed/uncertain/insufficient eligible -> “Mechanism evidence remains mixed or insufficient to distinguish the memory policies.”
- efficiency: matched measured curves favorable -> “The measured budget curves favor MORPH-FL within the studied range.” unfavorable -> “The measured budget curves favor a simpler control within the studied range.” mixed/uncertain -> “The budget curves show a tradeoff without consistent efficiency advantage.”
Figures budget-curves; tables primary-results, ablations; see FIGURE_SPECS. Unavailable: confidence calibration (null), dynamic causal validity, adjudicated root cause, human explanation quality, dollars. Existing helper functions do not establish measured endpoints.

## Binding and interpretation rules

All numeric/figure/table placeholders occur only in the Study/Results outlines. After the structural rewrite, claim slots appear only in the Results outline; the introduction ends with contributions and a roadmap. No numerical result is bound in this draft. Synthetic trace numbers are explicitly constructed policy inputs, not artifact-derived performance values. Configuration values and bibliography years are likewise not measured results.

The effectiveness claim slot has no automatic threshold for a global advantage. Its positive/negative/mixed alternatives above are author-reviewed syntheses of ALL declared contrasts, with conditions named. Statistical significance alone does not imply practical significance. Mechanism synthesis is qualitative; a positive narrative requires transition evidence plus compatible common-case component contrasts and must retain their confounds. If trajectories conflict or eligibility is insufficient, use the mixed/insufficient sentence. Cost synthesis uses matched subject curves and actual consumption; a lower ceiling or omitted interrupted cost cannot justify an efficiency sentence.

Additional interpretation-loss/suppression audit endpoints are planned, exploratory and unimplemented. Their units and denominators are defined in the Approach; no bound key or existing-retention-output claim is supplied for them. Semantic view sufficiency, calibrated confidence, dollar cost, human explanation quality and adjudicated causal validity remain unavailable.

Final RQ3 clarification: report descriptive ranking–cost profiles without a scalar price on tokens/probes/wall time or a binary universal “worth it” judgment. Favorable efficiency wording must identify the matched range/conditions and the no-worse-ranking/no-greater-reported-cost criterion; otherwise use the tradeoff alternative. Do not turn a cost preference selected after results into a registered decision rule. For BOTH exploratory audits, report total opportunities, assessable opportunities, events and unassessable counts; any percentage uses assessable opportunities. Suppression counts proposal occurrences, not unique logical IDs.
