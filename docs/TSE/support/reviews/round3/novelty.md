# Independent novelty and positioning review — round 3

Scope: current `main.tex`, Sections 1–4 (including abstract and closest-work table), `references.bib`, and targeted primary-source/code checks. I did not consult earlier reviews or writer-support material. This is a novelty/claim-boundary assessment, not an empirical-results review.

**Recommendation: Major Revision for a TSE research-paper submission in its present state; confidence: high on the positioning assessment, moderate on eventual significance.** The major-revision reason is that the paper's residual contribution is an empirical policy study whose findings are explicitly pending. It is not a finding that the front half currently makes a false novelty claim. Judged only as a method-and-positioning draft, Sections 1–4 need at most minor editorial refinement. I cannot recommend acceptance on the strength of a protocol and record-retention invariant, and I do not assume the eventual findings will be positive.

## Strengths

- The contribution is now sharply bounded: one scoped-assessment/portfolio/restart policy, rather than invention of agent memory, provenance, typed graphs, or forgetting. Introduction paragraphs beginning “The primary object” and “The results preview,” and the final positioning paragraph of Section 4, are appropriately explicit about this boundary.
- Ledger is recognized as a close predecessor, including deterministic execution state, rendered views, and redundant-action mediation. The claimed residual difference is a hypothesis portfolio and reset policy, not the external ledger itself. The primary Ledger description supports that distinction: its runtime view supplements persistent interaction history, whereas MORPH-FL clears role dialogues.
- Memory work is not erased by a narrow definition of within-task evidence. MemFL's project summaries and pseudo-training guidance and Repository Memory's historical commits/issues and active-file summaries are materially represented. The paper correctly treats those sources as different memory contents, not proof that prior localization systems lack memory.
- Claims track the controls. The observation-text condition is explicitly information-poorer as well as differently presented; the paper does not attribute its possible defeat to typed representation. Restricted Agentless/RGFL adaptations are not represented as complete upstream systems or as evidence of superiority over all closest work.
- Record retention, reconstructed-view sufficiency, and localization benefit are separated. Interpretation loss, logical-probe suppression, score accumulation from overlapping static checks, and unbounded observation views are disclosed as policy behavior. These qualifications prevent “evidence preserving” from silently becoming a claim of preserved reasoning or causal knowledge.

## Concerns, severity, and remedies

1. **Major, evidence pending rather than front-half incorrectness — Introduction contribution paragraph and Section 4 closing positioning.** The combination is plausibly distinct, but its pieces are established engineering mechanisms. With Ledger and discovery forgetting as close predecessors, executable composition alone does not yet establish TSE-level significance. The draft itself reaches the same conclusion. **Remedy:** complete the registered study and establish either useful reproducible policy effects at actual costs or a sufficiently informative, repeatable failure mechanism. A null average can be valuable, but needs explanatory evidence beyond “no significant difference.” This is required before acceptance, not a request to insert favorable claims now. **Type:** empirical significance.

2. **Minor, boundary to preserve — RQ2 and Sections 3.4/3.8.** Retaining a portfolio and changing reset timing also change live scores, available interpretations, and which logical probes remain suppressed. Thus even compatible component contrasts characterize this implementation's conditional behavior; they do not uniquely identify an anti-fixation mechanism or a general benefit from semantic diversity. The current prose largely says this correctly. **Remedy:** keep this restriction in eventual result interpretations and connect recovery cases to ordered proposals/assessments/suppression events, rather than reset counts. No new front-half architectural claim or content-matched control is mandatory while representation benefit remains expressly unclaimed. **Type:** interpretation/construct validity.

3. **Minor, optional positioning improvement — Section 4 memory/restart discussion.** The strongest simple comparator in the paper is a deterministic observation-text package. Lightweight trajectory-management work would further contextualize why such a comparator matters. Ledger itself discusses observation masking and compression alternatives. **Remedy:** consider a concise primary-source citation to a simple masking/context-management study, after verifying its method. This is an opportunity, not an identified priority dispute or a requirement to reproduce another full repair system. **Type:** related-work completeness.

## Questions and must-fix items

- What result pattern would make this policy useful enough to justify the extra state and heuristic choices, given that all observations can still grow the reconstructed request? Answer with measured ranking/cost/failure evidence, not an invariant.
- In eligible wrong-lead recoveries, did the retained alternative actually guide the subsequent investigation, or did fresh proposal generation recover the location despite lost links? The planned trajectory evidence should distinguish these explanations where possible.
- **Must fix before a finished-paper acceptance recommendation:** bind and audit the empirical evidence, retain the current package-level attribution, and prevent static-support/recovery results from becoming causal-certification or view-sufficiency claims. I found no separate major novelty correction required in Sections 1–4 as currently worded.
