## Strengths

- The operational specification is unusually candid. Attempt versus valid-round accounting, unchanged-prediction suppression, hypothesis recreation, unbounded observation views, additive-score dominance, and historical rather than leader-specific abstention are disclosed in places that let a reader interpret eventual outcomes.
- The running example connects symptom location to a plausible upstream operation without presenting a static match as a runtime experiment. The complete constructed reset gives checkable inputs, arithmetic, gates, and state effects. Its distance (0.88), token subtraction (16,000 minus 2,048), and affordability calculation are internally consistent.

Information omissions and reset-timing differences are explicit; typed formatting and a causal anti-fixation mechanism are not claimed to be identified. - Related Work locates the residual question narrowly, acknowledges external-memory predecessors, distinguishes executed adaptations from related systems, and avoids equating repair success with localization accuracy.

## Concerns and remedies

1. **Minor; definitional precision; Background, lines 95–99, versus Approach, lines 267–274.** Source presence is initially defined as a substring at a numbered line, but the Approach says an AST check can receive source-presence support and that AST checks search the whole file. A reader can consequently infer a stronger line-specific guarantee from the scope label than this pairing supplies. **Why it matters:** scope is the principal interpretive boundary between a checked fact and an explanation. **Remedy:** define source presence primarily by the predicate and explicitly say its spatial extent follows the producing procedure; distinguish line-local quote evidence from whole-file AST-derived text evidence. Alternatively state the exact line-specific restriction if it actually applies. **Must fix:** yes, as a small clarification to the completed definitions; no new experiment is required.

2. **Minor; evaluability; Introduction RQ3, lines 78–80.** “Earns its cost” does not specify how a reader should judge a ranking gain coupled with increased tokens, probes, or wall time. The later budget curves make the tradeoff observable but do not establish a unique favorable decision. **Why it matters:** competing costs can disagree, so a positive efficiency conclusion could otherwise depend on post-result preference. **Remedy:** state whether RQ3 will report descriptive tradeoffs/Pareto comparisons without a binary verdict, or identify the predeclared criterion for an “earns its cost” conclusion. **Must fix:** before interpreting RQ3 results; it need not become a new method or endpoint.

3. **Minor; exposition; Approach, especially Proposals/Execution, Reset/Reconstruction, and Ranking/Stopping.** The method is understandable, but its core loop is interleaved with many implementation exceptions. Some central facts must be assembled across sections: which identity is cached, which identity is handled, and which retained records feed scores versus the next model request. **Why it matters:** these distinctions are necessary to understand why a retained observation can remain visible while its score contribution disappears. **Remedy:** add a compact state-lifecycle summary or consolidate an existing table to show each state's identity, reset fate, and consumer. Preserve the disclosed exceptions. **Must fix:** no; editorial improvement.

4. **Minor; audit reporting precision; Approach, lines 535–541.** The exploratory event definitions are substantially evaluable, but the recreation denominator excludes incomplete artifacts whereas the suppression denominator is stated as all validated proposals. Missing artifacts are later marked unassessable without explicitly specifying suppression's assessed denominator. **Why it matters:** different missingness conventions can change the apparent event rate. **Remedy:** report total opportunities, assessable opportunities, events, and unassessable counts for each audit, and state which denominator produces any displayed percentage. **Must fix:** before audit frequencies are reported; the text already correctly requires frozen counting rules.

## Questions for authors

- Will RQ3 be a descriptive account of observed tradeoffs, or a decision under a declared cost preference? Either is defensible if stated before results interpretation.
- For an AST observation assessed with a substring prediction, what exact spatial claim does the source-presence scope authorize? The definition should answer this without requiring source inspection.
