\section{Threats to Validity}\label{sec:threats}
\subsection{Construct validity}
Patch changes are unreviewed proxies: a fix can touch supporting code or omit an indirectly responsible location. Source/AST observations establish static facts, not reproduced execution or causal diagnosis. Two issues lack function targets and are excluded from that metric only. Grounding and pool availability measure source admissibility and output, not truth. Scores and lexical/path diversity are heuristics, not calibrated probabilities. An unsupported proposal can outrank an uninvestigated file. Confidence is null, and abstention does not certify the leader.

\subsection{Internal validity}
The strongest contrast changes fields, provenance verbosity, dialogue, resets and pruning together. Ambiguity duplication is demonstrated but unquantified and affects richer requests. We retain frozen results and disclose this imbalance rather than infer a hypothesis-removal effect. Adaptive exposure differs by model; reacting to state does not randomize intervention opportunities. A shared controller controls engineering differences but supplies no independent-agent comparison.

Selection excluded recorded development IDs, but 132 Qwen predictions were reused and the preference for observation-only is outcome-informed. This is exploratory evidence, not untouched confirmation. Archived identity checks and receipts establish stated collection provenance; the local audit does not freshly verify all raw signatures. The October 28 checker-hardening metadata inspection was not replacement content-level verification. The release must include raw sources, configurations, receipts and permissible traces.

\subsection{External validity}
The sample contains fifty issues from twelve Python repositories, two approximately 7B-scale models and one seed. Balancing changes the benchmark mixture; neither whole splits nor an industrial population are represented. Both models use the same subjects. Public reports may leak fixes or have appeared in training~\citep{swebenchillusion2025,swebenchplus2024}. Pinning inputs does not remove contamination. Untouched/decontaminated subjects such as SWE-rebench test a different boundary~\citep{swerebench2025}. Other languages, sizes and execution evidence are outside the claims.

\subsection{Conclusion validity and reproducibility}
Paired counts are exact for saved outputs; generalization is uncertain. Intervals have twelve clusters, are unadjusted and descriptive. Zero discordance is not equivalence under other seeds or inputs. One seed does not estimate stochastic variation. Even without a reset, nondeterministic inference can change tokens from continuous context; we attribute no difference solely to a reset event. Exposure/pool analyses added in revision are descriptive, not retrospective protocol changes.

Resource means exclude interrupted/retried attempts, setup, energy and developer effort. Tokenizers differ. The local audit checks complete score keys, aggregates and tables; it does not reconstruct rankings from inaccessible raw model outputs. Exact evaluated dependencies and durable public release remain requirements. Shallow dataclass immutability and operational retention checks are not formal semantic-preservation proofs. These limits restrict inference without changing the recorded 400-investigation/100-control coverage.
